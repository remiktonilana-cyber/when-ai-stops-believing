# When Should AI Stop Believing?

## Can AI distinguish a temporary drawdown from the death of a profitable market belief?

An open benchmark studying how AI systems revise beliefs when previously successful patterns encounter changing environments.

## Research Question

When reality repeatedly contradicts a previously successful belief, how much evidence does an AI system need before changing its mind?

## Models

- DeepSeek
- GPT
- Grok
- Qwen
